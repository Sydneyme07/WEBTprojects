Classic Vanilla Buttercream

A smooth, creamy vanilla buttercream that is perfect for spreading, piping, and stacking layer cakes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • 2–3 tablespoons heavy cream or milk
  • 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Beat butter on medium speed for 2–3 minutes until smooth and creamy.
  2. Gradually add powdered sugar, mixing on low speed until combined.
  3. Add vanilla extract, salt, and 2 tablespoons cream.
  4. Beat on medium-high speed for 2–3 minutes until light and fluffy.
  5. Add additional cream, 1 tablespoon at a time, until desired consistency is reached.

Chocolate Buttercream

A rich and smooth chocolate buttercream with deep flavor and a soft, creamy texture ideal for layering and decorating.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 ½ cups powdered sugar
  • ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2–3 tablespoons heavy cream or milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Beat butter until smooth and creamy.
  2. Sift together powdered sugar and cocoa powder.
  3. Gradually add dry ingredients to butter, mixing on low speed.
  4. Add vanilla, salt, and 2 tablespoons cream.
  5. Beat on medium-high for 2–3 minutes until fluffy and smooth.
  6. Add more cream if needed to reach desired consistency.

Whipped Vanilla Frosting

A light, airy whipped frosting that is less sweet than traditional buttercream. Perfect for soft finishes and fresh fruit cakes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream (cold)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened (for stability)

Instructions

  1. Beat softened cream cheese until smooth.
  2. Add powdered sugar and mix until combined.
  3. Slowly pour in cold heavy cream while mixing on low speed.
  4. Add vanilla extract.
  5. Increase to medium-high speed and whip until stiff peaks form.
  6. Use immediately or refrigerate until ready to frost.

Whipped Chocolate Frosting

A fluffy chocolate whipped frosting with a smooth texture and rich cocoa flavor. Ideal for chocolate cakes or layered desserts.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream (cold)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ¼ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Beat softened cream cheese until smooth.
  2. Sift together powdered sugar and cocoa powder, then mix into cream cheese.
  3. Slowly add cold heavy cream while mixing on low speed.
  4. Add vanilla extract.
  5. Increase speed and whip until stiff peaks form.
  6. Refrigerate 20–30 minutes before piping for best structure.
