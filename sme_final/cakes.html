Classic Homemade Vanilla Cake

This from-scratch vanilla cake is soft, buttery, and perfectly balanced. It has a fine crumb and is sturdy enough for stacking and decorating.

Ingredients

Dry Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Wet Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whole milk (room temperature)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and line two 8-inch round cake pans with parchment paper.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, beat butter until smooth. Add sugar and beat for 3–4 minutes until light and fluffy.
  4. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stir in vanilla extract.
  5. Add dry ingredients in three parts, alternating with milk. Begin and end with dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
  6. Divide batter evenly between pans. Bake for 25–30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  7. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

Classic Chocolate Cake

A rich, deeply chocolate cake with a moist crumb. Perfect for layering with chocolate buttercream or ganache.

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ cups granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup hot coffee (or hot water)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and line two 8-inch cake pans.
  2. Whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Add eggs, oil, milk, and vanilla. Mix until smooth.
  4. Slowly stir in hot coffee. Batter will be thin — this is normal.
  5. Divide evenly between pans.
  6. Bake 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely before frosting.
