Essential Tools for Cake Decorating

  • Turntable: Rotates for easy access to all sides of the cake.
  • Offset Spatula: Used for spreading, smoothing, and lifting, with a 4-inch size being versatile.
  • Cake Leveler or Serrated Knife: Ensures even cake layers.
  • Piping Bags & Tips: Essential for icing, borders, and writing (e.g., star tip, round tip).
  • Icing Smoother/Scraper: A metal or acrylic scraper for smooth, clean sides.
  • Couplers: Allows changing piping tips without emptying the bag.
  • Cake Boards: Provide a stable base for transport and presentation.
